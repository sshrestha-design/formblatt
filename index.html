Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about creating fillable PDF forms with JustForms.

Is JustForms really 100% free?

Yes! JustForms is completely free to use. There are no subscriptions, paywalls, watermarks, page limits, or account registrations required.

Are my uploaded documents private and secure?

Absolutely. JustForms processes all PDF parsing, field placement, and AcroForm generation 100% locally in your web browser memory. Your documents and client data are never uploaded to any remote server or third-party cloud.

Will the generated fillable form work in Adobe Acrobat & Mac Preview?

Yes. JustForms exports strict ISO 32000 compliant PDF AcroForms. When clients open your PDF in Adobe Acrobat Reader, macOS Preview, Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or DocuSign, they can click, type, select dropdowns, check boxes, and sign seamlessly.

How does the Smart Auto-Detect feature work?

The Smart Auto-Detector analyzes the spatial layout and text geometry of your PDF to find labels, blank underlines (_______), and checkboxes ([ ]), automatically generating interactive form fields in under 30 milliseconds.

Privacy Policy

Zero-Server Data Protection Guarantee • Effective: August 2026
Executive Summary: JustForms operates 100% locally on your computer. We do not operate databases or cloud processing servers for your documents. Your files and form entries are never transmitted over the internet.

1. No Server-Side File Processing

When you open or drop a PDF into JustForms, the binary stream is decoded directly into your browser's private WebAssembly/JavaScript memory space via the open-source pdf.js and pdf-lib libraries. At no point are your PDF bytes sent to any remote server.

2. Zero Keystroke or Input Telemetry

We do not capture, record, or track keystrokes, form input values, radio choices, dropdown selections, or drawn signature graphics. All form manipulation takes place entirely within your client session.

3. Client-Side Local Storage & Auto-Save

JustForms may store temporary field coordinates and theme preferences in your browser's localStorage or sessionStorage to prevent work loss during accidental browser tab refreshes. This data resides strictly on your physical machine.

4. Independent Verification

You can verify our privacy guarantee at any time using your browser's Developer Tools Network Tab (F12) to confirm 0 bytes of outgoing document traffic.

Zero Tracking Cookies
Zero Third-Party Advertising Cookies: JustForms does not use marketing trackers, ad networks, tracking pixels, or cross-site fingerprinting cookies.

1. What Browser Storage Is Used?

JustForms only uses essential client-side web storage mechanisms (localStorage and sessionStorage) for core functional features:

  • Crash Guard & Session State: Preserving unsaved form layout in progress so accidental browser reloads do not discard your field placements.
  • Onboarding & User Preferences: Remembering whether you have completed the visual onboarding tour and tool preference choices.

2. Managing Your Storage

You can clear all stored local application state at any time through your browser's "Clear Site Data" or "Clear Cookies & Cache" settings without affecting future use.

Compliance & Security Architecture

HIPAA, GDPR & ISO 32000 Architectural Standards
HIPAA & Protected Health Information (PHI) Boundary: Because JustForms runs entirely as client-side software on your local machine and does not transmit, ingest, process, or store files on external cloud servers, JustForms is not a "Business Associate" under HIPAA. All Protected Health Information (PHI) created or entered into forms remains strictly under the direct physical and network control of your covered entity.

1. GDPR & Privacy by Design

JustForms adheres to the strictest principles of GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) by implementing Data Minimization and Privacy by Design. Since zero personal data is collected or transferred, user privacy is guaranteed by architectural design rather than trust.

2. ISO 32000-1 / PDF 1.7 Compliance

Forms generated by JustForms adhere to ISO 32000-1 specification standards for PDF interactive form widgets (/AcroForm dictionary, /Widget annotations, /DR resources, /V appearance streams). This ensures standard verification and signature validation in Adobe Acrobat and enterprise document management systems.
