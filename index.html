FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common questions & answers

Is Formblatt completely free?

Yes. There are no paid tiers, watermarks, trial expiration dates, or page limits.

Are my uploaded files private?

Yes. Documents load into local browser memory and never leave your machine. No files are uploaded to any server.

Will forms open properly in Acrobat and Preview?

Yes. Every file exports as an ISO 32000 compliant AcroForm, fully supported in Acrobat, Preview, Chrome, and Edge.

How does Auto-Detect work?

It scans underlying page vectors for form lines, bounding boxes, and checkboxes, then inserts interactive fields automatically.

Can I use Formblatt for confidential patient or legal records?

Yes. Because all PDF manipulation runs strictly in your local browser sandbox, no external server processes your data.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Formblatt runs entirely in your browser. Here's exactly what that means:

Your documents. When you open or drop in a PDF, it's parsed and edited locally using pdf.js and pdf-lib. No file data is sent to a server - you can confirm this yourself in your browser's Network tab (see the Security section on the homepage).

Hosting. The app itself (HTML, CSS, JS, and WebAssembly files) is served as static assets via Vercel. This is the same as any website - it's how you receive the app, not how your documents are handled.

Analytics. We use Vercel Analytics to see aggregate metrics like page load speed. This doesn't include your name, IP address tied to identity, or anything about the documents you work with.

Third parties. We don't use ad networks, trackers, or data brokers. Vercel is our only infrastructure provider.

Cookie and Storage Policy

Zero Advertising Cookies • Local Browser Storage Only
Zero Third-Party Advertising Cookies: Formblatt does not set marketing cookies, tracking pixels, or cross-site profiling trackers. The application is delivered via Vercel with anonymous development performance analytics.

1. What Browser Storage Is Used?

Formblatt only uses essential client-side web storage mechanisms (localStorage and sessionStorage) on your device for core functional features:

  • Crash Guard and Session State: Preserving unsaved form layout in progress so accidental browser reloads do not discard your field placements.
  • Onboarding and User Preferences: Remembering whether you have completed the visual onboarding tour and tool preference choices.

2. Managing Your Storage

You can clear all stored local application state at any time through your browser's "Clear Site Data" or "Clear Cookies and Cache" settings without affecting future use.

Compliance Notes

Last updated: August 27, 2026

HIPAA. Formblatt does not transmit or store any data on external servers. Because no Protected Health Information (PHI) ever leaves your browser, Formblatt does not process or hold your records. We recommend checking with your compliance team before handling covered healthcare documents.

GDPR. Since Formblatt does not collect, track, or transmit personal data, there is no server-side processing under GDPR. All operations stay strictly inside your local browser session.

PDF Standard. Forms exported by Formblatt follow the universal ISO 32000-1 AcroForm standard. This ensures full compatibility with Adobe Acrobat, Apple Preview, and modern web browsers. Inspect our open-source code on GitHub.

Your Responsibility. Formblatt runs entirely in your local browser sandbox. Your own device security, storage, and organizational policies govern how you store and share completed documents.
